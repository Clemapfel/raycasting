Welcome to the home page of **Chroma Void**, an upcoming indie platformer.

°trail title card°

Inspired the polygonal fluidity and time-attack-first nature of games like  <Link text="Dust Force" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/65300/Dustforce_DX/" /> and <Link test="N++" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/230270/N_NPLUSPLUS/" />, **Chroma Void** aims to provide deep, yet elegantly simple platforming mechanics. High-level gameplay focuses on speed and fluidity of movement over pixel-perfect precision and overwhelming obstacle or enemy placement.

°video of flowing gameplay°

Using state-of-the-art 2D physics simulation, **Chroma Void** allows the player to freely deform like rubber, allowing for mechanics rarely seen in platformers.
