Welcome to the home page of <RainbowLink text="Chroma Void" />, an upcoming indie platformer game.

Despite sharing the genre, <RainbowLink text="Chroma Void" /> aims to break with the traditions of challenging platformers by focusing on <Link text="flow" href="/posts/flow" /> over precision.

With a novel algorithm of measuring how well a player is navigating the levels, <RainbowLink text="Chroma Void" /> aims to put smoothness of player expression along with simple completion time as the main metrics players should strive for when completing a level.

The games control find depth in simplicity, with an elegant 2-button control screen.
